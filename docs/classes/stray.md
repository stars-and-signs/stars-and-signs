## Core Class Features

When you gain this Class, you gain the Hyperfixated and Creative Class Features.

### Lucky

You have a five slot Resource Counter called "Luck" with five slots, each worth 5 Luck. The referee maintains this Counter and keeps it secret. Whenever you suffer a Complication or Cost, you can expend Luck instead (no Action required). The referee rolls 1d10 and expends that much Luck.

When your Luck Counter fills, you suffer Fallout of the referee's choice and then clear all filled Luck slots. Clear all filled Luck slots at each Major Milestone.

### Resourceful

You have a Resource Counter called "Stash" with three slots. You can fill a slot to create an Asset of your description. Choose one Skill, the Asset can be used for Checks made using that Skill with a Potency of 1. To determine the Quality, roll 1d10 and divide by 2 (round up).

You can fill one unfilled Luck slot to clear a Stash slot. Your filled Stash slots are cleared at each Major Milestone.
