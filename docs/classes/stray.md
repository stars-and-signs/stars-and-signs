## Core Class Features

When you gain this Class, you gain the Lucky and Resourceful Class Features.

### Lucky

The referee gains a five-slot Resource Counter associated with you called "Luck". Each slot is worth 5 Luck. The referee maintains this Counter and keeps the number of slots filled secret from you.

Whenever you suffer a Complication or Cost, you can choose to expend 1d10 Luck instead (no Action required). The referee rolls this die secretly.

When your Luck Counter fills, you suffer Fallout of the referee's choice and then clear all filled Luck slots. Clear all filled Luck slots at each Major Milestone.

### Resourceful

You have a Resource Counter called "Stash" with three slots.

Whenever you score a Graze or better on a Check to Discover or Create a physical Asset, you can fill one Stash slot to upgrade that Asset. Each of the Asset's Functions have their Potency increased by 1, to a maximum of 2.

You may spend any amount of Effort in any combination of types to increase the Quality of this Asset. You can increase the Quality by 1 for every 5 points of Effort spent, to a maximum of Quality 5.

You can fill one unfilled Luck slot to clear a Stash slot. Your filled Stash slots are cleared at each Major Milestone.
