## Core Class Features

When you gain this Class, you gain the Hyperfixated and Creative Class Features.

### Hyperfixated

Choose one of your Affinities, it becomes your Hyperfixation. You can always invoke it when making a Check. When you invoke it, you gain both a Circumstance Boon and an Advantage die instead of only one.

### Creative

When you make a Create Check that benefits from an Advantage die, if you succeed, you can spend any amount of Sweat. You gain an Extra for every 5 points of Sweat spent.
