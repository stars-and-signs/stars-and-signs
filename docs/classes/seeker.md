## Core Class Features

When you gain this Class, you gain the Hyperfixated and Creative Class Features.

### Archivist

You have a five-slot Progress Counter called "Case File". Whenever you score a Hit or better on a Discover Check, once you learn the result, you can choose to dedicate your Case File to the subject of that Check. If you do, fill a slot. When you fill the last slot of your Case File Counter, clear it and gain a Temporary Positive Trait representing that subject.

When you make a Remember Test related to a subject for which you have a Case File Trait, you can invoke that Trait to automatically succeed on that Test.

You lose all Case File Traits at a Major Milestone.
